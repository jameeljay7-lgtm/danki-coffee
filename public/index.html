Coffee Science

Artisanal Brewing Guide

Simple recipes to get the most from your Danki Coffee.

French Press

Coarse · 60g/L · 4 mins

Pre-heat, saturate with 94°C water, stir and press gently after four minutes.

Pour Over (V60)

Medium-Fine · 15g/250g · 3 mins

Rinse the filter, bloom for 30 seconds, then pour steadily in concentric circles.

Espresso

Fine · 18g/36g · 28–32 secs

Tamp evenly and aim for a balanced extraction around thirty seconds.

Moka Pot

Medium · Fill basket · 5 mins

Use hot water in the lower chamber, don't pack the coffee, and brew over low heat.

The Danki Story

Danki Coffee was born out of a profound love for Tanzanian coffee heritage. Sourced from local farming communities in the Southern Highlands and Northern slopes of Kilimanjaro, the Danki team roasts every batch in Dar es Salaam to preserve the rich, sweet caramel tones and vibrant citrus notes of East African Arabica.
