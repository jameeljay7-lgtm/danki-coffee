Our Story

Born in the
Highlands.

Danki Coffee was founded with one mission — to bring the world-class coffee grown in Tanzania's Southern Highlands directly to your cup, without compromise.

Our beans are sourced from small farms at altitudes above 1,500m, where the cool mountain air and rich volcanic soil create the perfect growing conditions. We roast in small batches to preserve every note.
