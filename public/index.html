Coffee Science

Artisanal Brewing Guide

Maximize the flavors of Danki Coffee beans with our recommended recipes.

French Press

  • Grind: Coarse
  • Ratio: 60g / Litre
  • Brew: 4 mins

Pre-heat press. Add coffee, saturate with hot water (94°C) and stir. Press gently after 4 mins.

Pour Over (V60)

  • Grind: Medium-Fine
  • Ratio: 15g / 250g
  • Brew: 3 mins

Rinse filter paper. Bloom for 30 seconds, then complete the pour with a steady concentric stream.

Espresso

  • Grind: Fine
  • Ratio: 18g / 36g
  • Brew: 28-32 secs

Tamp evenly. Aim for 36g of liquid extraction within 30 seconds for optimal crema and sweetness.

Moka Pot

  • Grind: Medium
  • Ratio: Fill basket
  • Brew: 5 mins

Fill bottom chamber with hot water to valve. Add coffee without packing. Brew over low heat.

The Danki Story

Danki Coffee was born out of a profound love for Tanzanian coffee heritage. Sourced directly from local farming communities in the Southern Highlands and Northern slopes of Kilimanjaro, Catherine Kikkides and the Danki team roast every batch by hand in Dar es Salaam to preserve the rich, sweet caramel tones and vibrant citrus notes of 100% East African Arabica.
